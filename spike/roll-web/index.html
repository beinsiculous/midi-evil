The editor's keys

Keys

Tab into the editor. F6 moves between the transport and the piano roll. In the roll, Left and Right move between notes, Home and End to the first and last, Up and Down change pitch (with Shift, an octave), Control with Left or Right moves a note, Shift with Left or Right changes its length, Enter adds a note and Delete removes one. Tab from the roll leaves the editor; Shift+Tab from the transport leaves it backwards.
